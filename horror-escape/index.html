How to play

  1. The brass key is hidden in one room of the house. It changes every game.
  2. Walk with WASD and look around with the arrow keys.
  3. When you can see the key on screen, face it and press E to grab it.
  4. To change rooms, face a doorway and press F, then pick a room (1–4).
  5. Lost? Press Space. Something in the house will whisper which way to go.
  6. With the key, return to the foyer, press F and choose The front door.

You have 2 minutes. The clock stops while a room loads. Expect company.
